LockFrame Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The short version

LockFrame doesn't collect, store off the phone, share or sell any personal data. It has no account system and no internet access permission.

Camera and microphone

LockFrame uses the camera and microphone only while you run a sync test or a calibration. Frames and audio are analysed in the phone's memory to find flashes, beeps and knocks, then discarded. Nothing is recorded to a file, and nothing leaves the phone.

Motion sensor

During the knock check, LockFrame reads the phone's accelerometer to time each knock. The readings are used on the phone and discarded.

What is stored on the phone

  • Test results (offset readings and their times), so you can see your last result.
  • The phone's calibration value.
  • Your settings, and how many free tests you've used.

These stay in the app's private storage, and the app opts out of Android cloud backup. Settings › Delete all data removes your results, calibration and settings; the free-test count is kept so the free allowance can't be reset. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Purchases

LockFrame is free during the beta. If a paid unlock is offered later, it will be bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and LockFrame only receives confirmation that the unlock was purchased, not your payment details.

Diagnostics you choose to share

Beta builds keep a measurement log on the phone (timings of detected flashes, beeps and knocks, and the results). It contains no audio, pictures or personal details. It only leaves the phone if you tap Settings › Share diagnostics and choose where to send it.

Crash reports

If you've allowed it in Android, Google may share anonymous crash and performance statistics with developers through Google Play. LockFrame itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If the app crashes, it keeps a short note of the error on the phone so it can offer to recover next time; that note stays on the phone unless you include it when sharing diagnostics.

Children

LockFrame isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
